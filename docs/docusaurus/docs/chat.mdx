A Chat is a direct conversation with any supported coding agent, without workflow steps or approvals. Move it into the workflow when it grows.

<Screenshot name="chat" alt="Chat with its conversation, task controls and composer" caption="An example task in a fictional project." />

## Start a Chat {#start}

Choose **+ › Chat**, pick your project and describe what you need. **Conversation** and **Terminal** show the same session. Return to the task to keep its history. The agent's menu in the header has **Interrupt**, **Stop session** and **Resume session**.

<Tip>To continue a session you started outside QualityLayer, choose it under **Or resume a session in Claude Code** (or Codex) below the card. It opens as a Chat.</Tip>

## Questions and tasks {#questions}

Your agent's questions appear above the composer: pick a numbered choice or type your answer. When the agent reports a task list, **Tasks** shows its progress. A Codex session started outside QualityLayer is read-only here; answer it in Codex.

## Add what you need {#tools}

Ask your agent to save a plan, make [artifacts](artifacts.mdx), build in slices, run checks or ask for an independent review. Saved plans open in the Reader, artifacts in their viewer, check results in the conversation.

## Convert to task {#convert-to-task}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Convert">Choose **Convert to task** in the header, then **Feature** or **Bugfix**.</Step>
  <Step title="Pick the start">**Plan with QualityLayer** continues in Plan with the conversation and saved work as context. **Saved plan**, then **Start at the Plan**, maps a plan this Chat saved into the Plan; the original stays unchanged.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve the Plan">As for any task, before the build. Let any running helpers finish, or stop them, first.</Step>
</Steps>

Both choices keep the same task and agent conversation.

<Note>A Chat stays on your computer. Reach it from elsewhere through a [machine](machines.mdx) you added, or a browser paired with `qualitylayer app pair`.</Note>
