## Plan, build and review {#steps}

<Screenshot name="new-task" alt="New task in the App: Feature or Bugfix, the request, where the agent runs, and the planner as agent, model and effort" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the change">Choose **+ › Task**, then **Feature** or **Bugfix**. Describe the change, attach files if needed, then choose your agent and where it runs.</Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">They come one at a time in **Your turn**. Each answer reaches your agent at once.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve the Plan">Read it, then choose **Approve and build here**, or **Ask agent** to change something. **Choose where to build** picks another agent or workspace.</Step>
  <Step title="Let it build">Follow Implement and Verify, or do something else. Anything only you can do reaches you as one question.</Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve">On Verify, **Review the code**, see what changed and how **Done means** ran, then approve.</Step>
  <Step title="Merge">Your agent opens the pull request. You merge it on GitHub.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>To reuse a plan you already have, choose **Existing plan**, paste or attach it, then **Start from this plan** ([details](steps/plan.mdx#a-bug)). **+ › Chat** starts a conversation without steps ([Chat](chat.mdx)).</Tip>

## Where your agent runs

**New task** and **Start the build** both ask.

<Tabs groupId="where">
  <Tab value="app" label="In the App">Recommended. Pick the agent, model, effort, project and branch. Questions and requests to your agent happen in the App.</Tab>
  <Tab value="agent" label="In my own agent">Ask your agent in your terminal, IDE or agent app in your own words, or copy the prepared prompt; attached files are listed by path. The agent asks in its own chat. You can still approve in the App, or [work only in your agent](work-in-your-agent.mdx).</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Good to know {#good-to-know}

- Say QualityLayer or QL when you ask your agent to start work. Follow-up answers continue the task; no slash command is needed.
- Select a passage and choose **Ask agent**. Edit the draft in Conversation, then send it.
- You can stop or pause the run. Your agent's own permission and sign-in prompts still need you.
- To continue elsewhere, stop the run and ask: "Using QL, continue task retry-webhooks." The Plan and evidence stay with the task.
- With several approved Plans waiting, "Implement this using QualityLayer" lists their commands.
