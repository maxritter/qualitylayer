title: Artifacts
description: Create and review mockups, diagrams and clickable pages with your agent, then keep local revisions.
---

An artifact is a mockup, diagram or clickable set of pages you work on with your agent. A **draft** belongs to one task; a **lasting artifact** is kept for the project.

## Create and review

Ask “Using QL, show two layouts for the settings page.” No workflow starts. A task's draft appears as a compact entry in **Files**.

With **In my terminal**, **Create and copy** creates the artifact and copies its request. The folder chip copies its path. Paste into any supported agent, or ask in your own words.

### Ask about a spot {#full-size}

Choose a spot and **Ask agent**. Conversation opens with an editable request containing the artifact, page, spot and revision. Check it, say what should change and send through your agent's native input. Its answer appears in the genuine conversation. If Conversation input is unavailable, use the explicit terminal handoff. Selecting a spot never sends a message by itself.

Switch **Light**, **Dark**, **Desktop** and **Phone**; **F** goes full screen. Buttons and links work, and pages may run their own scripts, but nothing loads from the network. Your agent checks each page in both themes and widths before you see it.

## The Artifacts place {#artifacts-place}

**Artifacts** in the sidebar holds the **Library**. Search by project, artifact or page, narrow with **Filters**, and switch **List** or **Gallery**. Starred **Favorites** appear above the list. The row menu renames, copies, archives or deletes; **Archived › Recently deleted** restores.

Artifacts and their source stay on machines you control. Copies are independent. A listing in another local project does not upload the artifact or give another person access.

## Lasting artifacts {#lasting}

### Keep a draft {#keep}

**Save to library…** in a draft's menu stores it **In the repository** (travels with commits) or **Only on this computer** (out of Git). To change a lasting artifact, ask your agent to draft on it. **Files** keeps its source, metadata and revision actions together. Restoring a revision preserves the artifact's identity; deleting a registration never deletes the application source it points to.

### Fold in at Review {#fold-in}

The original stays until Review shows **This task changes …**: pick the pages (**Compare** shows both), then **Fold N pages in**. They join the pull request only with your authorized Git action.

## Existing artifacts

Local files and revisions remain usable after upgrading. Existing external-link metadata can remain as history; it grants no hosted publication or comment-import capability. Earlier private annotations and attachments are retained as read-only local records, without replaying them to an agent.
