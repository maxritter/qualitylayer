# Approved deliberate feedback on artifacts

Max approved the Opus 5.5 high-effort design on 16 November 2026: “Alright, looks good. Please implement it like that”. The App Model page `artifact-feedback.html` shows it.

On Plan, Review and standalone artifact pages, the person marks feedback deliberately. The Feedback button arms one spot or area mark; selected words offer a Feedback chip. Plain clicks, drags and selections use the artifact itself.

Marks reach the agent through the existing quick field and feedback record, with the same content as before: kind (spot, region or text), page, section label, quote and position. The workflow, its gates and the person's approvals are unchanged; a mark never approves or amends a plan by itself.
