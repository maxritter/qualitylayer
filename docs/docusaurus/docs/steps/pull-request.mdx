Open a pull request when you approve on [Verify](verify.mdx#approve). Your agent follows its checks and comments; you merge.

<Screenshot name="pull-request" alt="The merged pull request: its checks, its reviewers and the time from the first question to the approval" caption="An example task in a fictional project." />

## How Verify follows it {#following}

While the App is open, Verify reads checks and reviewers through `gh`. Without it, the task stays open until a pull or fetch brings the merge into local Git, or you mark it merged.

## Your agent watches the checks {#watch}

Your agent runs `qualitylayer pr watch` until the checks pass:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="The checks pass" icon="badge-check">It checks open review threads and reads new comments, and asks you before changing anything they request.</Card>
  <Card title="A check fails" icon="triangle-alert">It reads the log, fixes the cause, pushes and watches again. It never weakens or skips a check.</Card>
  <Card title="The branch conflicts" icon="git-pull-request">It merges or rebases the way your repository does, checks that no commit was lost, then pushes.</Card>
  <Card title="Three attempts used" icon="info">It reports what still fails and stops.</Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>Your agent never force-pushes commits that are not its own.</Note>

## When something waits for you {#waits}

![Review threads and failing checks come back to you; you push the fix for review threads; you merge](pathname:///img/diagrams/pull-request-light.svg)
![Review threads and failing checks come back to you; you push the fix for review threads; you merge](pathname:///img/diagrams/pull-request-dark.svg)

Failing checks, review threads and conflicts appear in **Needs you** while the App is open. [Watch pull requests](../reference/settings.mdx#git-and-editor) can wake or resume your agent, also for new comments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask your agent">"Using QL, review task retry-webhooks", with your task's name.</Step>
  <Step title="It fixes the routine requests">Test first, against the code and the approved Plan. Scope or design changes, and replies ready to post, come to you.</Step>
  <Step title="Push the fix">For review threads, the push is yours: press **Push the fix**. Your replies post on GitHub in your name.</Step>
</Steps>

## When you merge {#merged}

When you merge, the task moves to **Shipped** with what it cost. A squash or rebase merge is recognized too, and **Mark as merged** covers any other case. Its documents stay in `docs/plans/`. Further work starts with **Start a follow-up**.
